01

discovery workshop

Sit with the founders and agree on the problem, the audience and what is out of scope, before anything gets drawn.

02

research, then synthesis

Interviews and usability tests, clustered into the few themes that actually deserve a design decision.

03

design on a system

Wireframes up to high fidelity, built on a component library the team can reuse next sprint.

04

ship, then look again

Developer handoff or the CMS build myself, then watch the numbers and fix what they tell me.
