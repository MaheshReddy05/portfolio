01 / About the client

Healthier, wealthier and longer, one lesson at a time.

Optimity is a micro-learning app that helps millions of people live healthier, wealthier, and longer. If you're looking to improve your health and wellness, it's essential that you get the information you need, personalized to your specific lifestyle and habits. Optimity does just that by taking data from your wearables and combining it with information from your lifestyle, to provide actionable insights. As a result, this system can help improve your health and improve your quality of life.

Our team was delighted to be given the opportunity to create a website that showcases Optimity's brand and value proposition, helping to bring in new B2B clients.

02 / Project goals and requirements

Nail the brand, sell the USP, land B2B clients.

We were tasked with creating a website that nails Optimity's branding, brings out its USP, and helps it land new B2B clients. Our primary objective was to collaborate with Optimity and contribute to their B2B growth by developing supplementary webpages. 06 / Design system

One set of parts for every page.

As part of our project, we used the spark method, a comprehensive design system that we created specifically for this project. This involved developing a set of consistent design elements, such as typography, colour palettes, and button styles, that would be applied consistently across all webpages. By establishing a cohesive design system, we aimed to ensure visual harmony, improve user experience, and facilitate efficient collaboration throughout the project.

This design system served as a foundation for maintaining brand consistency and providing a polished and cohesive UI for this next stage of Optimity's B2B web presence.

Using our design system, we aimed to create visually compelling and engaging designs that aligned seamlessly with Optimity's brand identity. This phase allowed us to bring the project to life visually, ensuring that the end product would not only be visually appealing but also functional and user-friendly.
