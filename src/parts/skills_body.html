Skill files

Plain markdown files that hand an AI tool the parts of my job I keep repeating. They are not tied to one assistant: the same file works wherever I am working that week. Both of these are in daily use.

design-system-extractor.skill.md Point it at a screenshot, a URL or a moodboard and it comes back with a whole token set: colour ramps checked for contrast, a type scale, spacing, radii and shadows, written out as CSS variables. markdown, any tool
drd-writer.skill.md Reads a pile of messy call notes and returns a DRD or a PRD: the problem, who it is for, what is in and out of scope, how we will know it worked, and the questions nobody answered yet. markdown, any tool
