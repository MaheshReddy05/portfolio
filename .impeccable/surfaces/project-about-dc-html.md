Scope: the About page of Mahesh's portfolio, a new artboard (project/About.dc.html) on the existing Design canvas. Visitor mode: read.

Audience: hiring managers and recruiters, clients and collaborators, fellow designers, weighted equally. Job: understand who Mahesh is, how he works, and what he has done, then reach him. Proof/content: career history, education and tools are bracketed placeholders until Mahesh supplies them; nothing invented. Constraints: inherits the homepage world (tokens, type, motion, companion cursor, grain, light default with dark toggle); one terracotta CTA per page; no eyebrow labels above headings.

Chosen direction: The Studio Wall (locked on the decision page). Memorable moment: the Messy/Tidy switch reorganising the whole wall into a CV in one continuous motion.

Unresolved: real portrait, real roles/companies/years, education, awards/talks, tool list, CV file, whether artboard links between Main and About navigate in the published view.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The About page is Mahesh's studio wall: pinned paper artifacts a visitor can pick up and move, and one Messy/Tidy switch that snaps the entire wall into a clean, skimmable CV. It refuses the category default of portrait, bio paragraph and vertical timeline.

OWN-WORLD: Inherited linen and aubergine: sunken linen board on the canvas ground, raised paper artifacts with tape, a polaroid, ruled index cards with a rose margin line, perforated ticket stubs for roles, a cinema ticket, an illustrated comic cover with halftone, a sticker sheet of petrol-edged tools, a peach sticky note in olive for "currently". Bricolage display, Instrument Sans body, Caveat marginalia.

STORY: The visitor meets Mahesh in one line, plays with the wall, flips to Tidy to skim experience, principles, toolkit and learning, then says hello or downloads the CV.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Top left, "Hi, I'm Mahesh." at display size over a two-sentence intro; beneath it the Messy/Tidy segmented switch as the primary control with Download CV beside it. The wall board starts directly below at full content width, the polaroid, sticky note and three index cards visible above the fold, stubs peeking in.

FORM: The Studio Wall, candidate 2 of my ranked seven, seed key d930cf34. Signature interaction: FLIP transition of every artifact between scattered wall and ordered CV, translate plus rotation, 900ms ease-out, staggered.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
